STUDENT HOUSING LEASE

Parties: Campus Corner Homes ("Lessor") and the students signing below ("Lessees"), jointly and severally. This is a sample lease created for testing ClauseCheck; any resemblance to a real property is coincidental.

Section 1. Premises. Lessor leases to Lessees the house at 2200 Catlin Ave, Superior, WI, including the yard and driveway.

Section 2. Rent. Total rent is $2,100 per month, split among Lessees as they choose, payable to Lessor on or before the 1st.

Section 3. Deposit. Lessees will pay a deposit equal to one month's rent. Lessor shall refund the deposit within thirty (30) days of the end of the lease. Lessor will charge a cleaning fee of $250 and a repainting charge of $300, both to be withheld from the deposit.

Section 4. Snow and Lawn. Lessees are responsible for shoveling sidewalks within 24 hours of snowfall and mowing the lawn.

Section 5. Legal Costs. In the event of any dispute arising under this lease, Lessees agree to reimburse Lessor for all legal fees and court costs.

Section 6. Repairs. Lessor will repair major systems including the furnace, plumbing and electrical. Lessees will pay for repairs caused by their own misuse.

Section 7. Holding Over. If Lessees remain after the lease ends without written permission, rent will be charged at 150% of the monthly rate.

Section 8. Renewal. This lease does not automatically renew.
