APARTMENT RENTAL CONTRACT

Between Harbor View Management ("Management") and Resident. Sample document for testing ClauseCheck. It deliberately words problem clauses in unusual ways, so simple keyword matching misses them.

1. PAYMENT. Resident pays $990 on the first of every month.

2. MOVING OUT EARLY. Should Resident leave before the end date, every remaining monthly payment through the end date is owed to Management right away, and Management need not look for a replacement resident.

3. ACCESS. If Resident is more than a week behind on payment, Management may deny Resident access to the apartment until the balance is cleared.

4. INJURIES. Resident releases Management from responsibility for any harm suffered by Resident or guests anywhere on the property, even if Management caused it through carelessness.

5. ENFORCEMENT COSTS. Any expense Management pays a lawyer to enforce this contract will be added to Resident's account.

6. DEPOSIT. The deposit is returned within 21 days after Resident moves out, less lawful deductions.

7. MAINTENANCE. Resident agrees to take the apartment in its current condition and to handle and pay for every repair the apartment needs while Resident lives there.

8. NOISE. Quiet hours are 10 p.m. to 7 a.m.
