RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This sample agreement is between Lakeview Rentals ("Landlord") and the Tenant named below for a two-bedroom apartment in Superior, Wisconsin. It was written for testing ClauseCheck and is not a real lease.

1. TERM. The tenancy runs from August 15 through July 31.

2. RENT. Monthly rent is $875, due on the first day of each month. Rent may be paid by check or through the online portal at no extra charge.

3. LATE CHARGE. If rent is not received by the 5th of the month, a one-time late charge of $40 applies.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant will pay a security deposit of $875. Landlord will return the deposit, minus any lawful deductions for damage beyond normal wear and tear or unpaid rent, within 21 days after the tenancy ends, together with an itemized statement of any amounts withheld.

5. CHECK-IN. Tenant will receive a check-in sheet and has 7 days to note any existing damage or defects in the unit.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Landlord will keep the premises in a reasonable state of repair and comply with local housing codes. Tenant will keep the unit reasonably clean, replace light bulbs and furnace filters, and promptly report any needed repairs.

7. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the unit at reasonable times to inspect, make repairs, or show the unit, after giving at least 12 hours' advance notice, except in an emergency.

8. UTILITIES. Landlord pays water, sewer and trash. Tenant pays electricity and internet.

9. ENDING THE TENANCY. If either party wants to end the tenancy, notice must be given in writing as required by Wisconsin law. Any removal of a Tenant will occur only through a court eviction proceeding.

10. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenant moves out early, Landlord will make reasonable efforts to re-rent the unit, and Tenant will owe rent only until the unit is re-rented or the lease ends, whichever comes first.

11. PETS. One cat or one dog under 40 pounds is allowed with written approval.

12. INSURANCE. Tenant is encouraged to carry renter's insurance to cover personal belongings.

13. NOTICE. Domestic abuse protections: a tenant who is a victim of domestic abuse, sexual assault, or stalking may have special rights to terminate this lease under Wisconsin law.
