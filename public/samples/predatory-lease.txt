RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement is entered into between Northwoods Property Group LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned resident(s) ("Tenant") for the premises at 1418 Belknap St, Apt 3, Superior, Wisconsin. This is a sample document created for testing ClauseCheck and does not describe a real property.

1. TERM. The lease term begins September 1 and ends May 31.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay rent of $1,050 per month, due on the first day of each month, by electronic payment through the resident portal.

3. LATE FEES. Rent received after the 3rd of the month is late. A late fee of $25 per day will be charged until rent is paid in full.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,050 before move-in. Landlord will return the security deposit within 45 days after Tenant vacates the premises. A $175 professional carpet cleaning fee will be deducted from the deposit at move-out. Landlord may deduct for normal wear and tear on paint and flooring.

5. DEFAULT AND ACCELERATION. If Tenant fails to pay rent when due, the entire balance of rent for the remainder of the lease term shall immediately become due and payable. Landlord shall not be obligated to re-rent the premises.

6. LANDLORD REMEDIES. If rent is more than five days late, Landlord may change the locks and remove Tenant's belongings from the premises without further notice.

7. ATTORNEY FEES. Tenant shall pay all of Landlord's attorney's fees and collection costs incurred in enforcing this lease, whether or not a lawsuit is filed.

8. CONFESSION OF JUDGMENT. Tenant hereby authorizes any attorney to appear on Tenant's behalf and confess judgment against Tenant in favor of Landlord for all amounts due under this lease.

9. CONDITION OF PREMISES. Tenant has inspected the unit and accepts the premises as-is. Tenant shall make all repairs to the unit during the lease term at Tenant's own expense.

10. LIABILITY. Landlord shall not be liable for any injury, loss, or damage to persons or property occurring on the premises, including injury or damage caused by Landlord's own negligence.

11. DAMAGE. Tenant is responsible for all damage to the unit and building regardless of cause, including damage from fire, flood, or storm.

12. CRIMINAL ACTIVITY. Landlord may terminate this lease upon three days' notice if any crime occurs on the premises.

13. ENTRY. Landlord and Landlord's agents may enter the unit at any time, with or without notice, for any purpose.

14. GUESTS. Guests may not stay overnight more than two nights per month without Landlord's written approval.

15. COMPLAINTS. If Tenant contacts the building inspector or health department about the unit, Landlord may terminate this lease.

16. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This lease is the entire agreement between the parties.
